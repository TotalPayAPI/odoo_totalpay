TotalPay

Accept payments on your Odoo site in a seamless and secure checkout environment.

TotalPay integrates directly into Odoo's payment flow, giving your customers a fast, secure hosted checkout experience — backed by real-time payment confirmation, refunds, and automatic reconciliation.

Key Features

  • Secure Hosted Checkout — customers are redirected to TotalPay's secure payment page to complete their purchase.
  • Real-Time Confirmation — payments are confirmed automatically via secure server-to-server webhook, with a built-in fallback check if the webhook is delayed.
  • Full & Partial Refunds — issue refunds directly from Odoo's payment transaction screen.
  • Manual Capture (DMS Mode) — optionally authorize now and capture payment later.
  • Automatic Reconciliation — a built-in safety check ensures no payment confirmation is ever permanently missed.

Requirements

  • Odoo 19.0
  • An active TotalPay merchant account
  • A publicly reachable HTTPS domain for your Odoo instance
